Cafe Cart

The premise of this game is that your character starts out with just a food cart and a small apartment. From there they can buy ingredients from the grocery store, grow certain things to use as ingredients, and research to develop new dishes. This is the part of the game that will be a little different. To unlock abilities to grow a plant or cook a dish the player will have to answer certain questions about such things, if a player doesn't know the answer, we will have videos or documents to read that contain them but also teach them how to do such things in real life.
